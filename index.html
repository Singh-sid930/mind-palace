The Palace of Mind

a walkable chronicle of everything you have learned

Welcome in. This is a memory palace: a castle where every idea you have studied is given a room of its own to stand in. We remember places far better than we remember lists — so here the ideas have floors, doorways and neighbours. The mathematics that everything rests upon is down in the cellar, and each floor above is built from the one beneath it.

Nothing in these rooms is ornament. Every artifact is a working model of the idea it explains, and it moves the way the mathematics moves.

Walk with WASD, look with the mouse, hold Shift to run. When something catches your eye, press E to study it — plaques, figures and diagrams all open where you stand.

If you lose your bearings, M draws the floor you are on, G lifts the whole palace into a constellation of its ideas, and F carries you to any chamber you can name. Glowing footprints lead to whatever you have not read yet — P sends them away when you would rather wander. T calls Gemma, the ghost who knows which room you are standing in. N begins the music; wear headphones if you have them, as one layer of it exists only between the two ears.
